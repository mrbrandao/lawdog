# Notificação Extrajudicial

Verified: 2026-06-02
Sources: Lei 6.015/73 Art. 160; CC Art. 397; CPC Art. 726; Constituição Art. 5º LV.
Reference: https://www.jusbrasil.com.br/artigos/notificacao-extrajudicial-o-que-e

## What it is

A formal act registered at a Cartório de Títulos e Documentos (or sent via
certified channels) that officially notifies a party of a legal obligation,
fact, or claim. It has the legal force of a public record (fé pública) when
done at a cartório.

## Where to do it

| Channel | Legal strength | Cost | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cartório de Títulos e Documentos | Highest — fé pública, valid from any comarca | R$180–250 | 3–15 days delivery |
| Correios (Aviso de Recebimento — AR) | Good — proves delivery, no fé pública | R$30–50 | 2–7 days |
| Certified digital platform (ICP-Brasil + timestamp) | Growing acceptance, legally valid | Lower than cartório | 1–3 days |
| WhatsApp / email (no ICP-Brasil) | Fragile — easy to dispute | Negligible | Immediate |

**Recommendation:** Cartório or ICP-Brasil certified platform. Avoid unverified
email or WhatsApp — they are legally fragile.

## Legal Effects

- **Constitutes in mora (Art. 397, CC):** formal notice that the party is in
  default from the date of receipt
- **Interrupts prescription:** the statute of limitations clock resets (important
  in cases near expiry)
- **Creates formal record:** irrefutable proof that the party was notified on a
  specific date
- **Does NOT compel response:** silence is not automatically treated as admission
- **Does NOT replace judicial process:** it is a pre-step that often resolves
  disputes or strengthens the subsequent JEC case

## Response Timeline

No fixed legal deadline exists. The notifying party sets the term. Common practice:
- **15 days:** short, urgent matters (debt collection, immediate compliance)
- **30 days:** standard for most consumer and contract disputes
- **45–60 days:** complex matters or when good faith negotiation is expected

Absence of response does not create automatic legal consequences but:
- Demonstrates inaction — strengthens the judicial case
- Allows immediate filing in JEC (no further waiting required)

## Contranotificação

The other party may respond with a formal counter-notification (contranotificação).
- No mandatory deadline, but 15 business days is standard by analogy (CPC)
- Legal basis: Constitution Art. 5º LV, CPC Art. 726
- It documents their disagreement and may preview the defense arguments
- Does not stop or invalidate the original notification

Evaluate whether the contranotificação resolves the issue, disputes facts,
or is purely delaying. See `protocols/case-lifecycle.md` for orientation rules.

## When to Recommend (Before JEC)

**Recommend when:**
- Relationship is still viable and recent (< 90 days from the incident)
- Defendant is identified, known address or registered business
- Dispute value justifies cost (> R$500 rule of thumb, or cost < 20% of dispute)
- Realistic chance of amicable resolution

**Do NOT recommend when:**
- Conflict is already escalated or irreconcilable
- Defendant is unknown, bankrupt, or has history of ignoring notifications
- Dispute value is very low relative to cartório cost
- Urgency requires immediate judicial action (risk of evidence disappearing)

## In the JEC Context

- Not legally required before JEC — parties may file directly
- Prior notification strengthens the case: demonstrates good faith and due diligence
- May influence judge's view on costs/fees if the other party ignored it
- In rare cases (despejo, reintegração de posse), prior notification IS mandatory
  by law — but those cases do not typically go through JEC
